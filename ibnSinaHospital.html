Site Intelligence

A medical campus shaped byaccess and environmental logic.

The analysis boards highlight how the hospital site connects to major streets, benefits from a large and flexible footprint, and can absorb future medical growth while preserving visibility, circulation, and landscape quality.

Location

The site sits within a connected urban edge, allowing the hospital to read as a visible civic anchor with clear regional accessibility.

Environmental Response

Orientation, material strategy, roof reflectivity, and operable openings all support cooler, healthier internal conditions.

Landscape Buffer

Existing vegetation and planted edges help soften the clinical footprint while improving privacy, shade, and public perception.

Project Showcase

Clinical massing and facade views presented through a cleaner architectural sequence.

These renderings extend the project narrative through exterior viewpoints that emphasize proportion, entry presence, facade rhythm, and the hospital's visual identity within its site.

Four curated perspectives present the hospital as a composed civic landmark rather than a purely technical object.

Technical Portfolio

Plans, sections, and elevations organized like a composed hospital case study.

The drawings reveal a layered medical program with outpatient, inpatient, administration, and service components arranged through a disciplined structural and circulation framework.

The layout mixes a large master drawing with smaller supporting sheets so the presentation feels clinical, ordered, and premium rather than crowded.
