Project 04
Hospitality Design 3D Modeling Technical Drawings

4 Star Hotel

Smooth, elegant hospitality shaped through landscape and light.

The hotel is presented as a carefully staged arrival experience where circulation, water, planting, and faceted building volumes create a calm and contemporary destination with both civic presence and guest comfort.

Smooth, Elegant And Comfortable

The project frames comfort as an architectural sequence, combining quiet room wings, open leisure surfaces, and an expressive envelope that gives the hotel a memorable identity day and night.

3D Modeling

Structural expression and roof geometry support the hotel identity.

The modeling studies reveal a project language built around framed spans, tensile forms, and layered roof movement. These pieces help explain how the hotel combines decorative elegance with a disciplined structural logic.

Envelope Character

Perforated surfaces, faceted screens, and sculpted vertical elements give the hotel a refined silhouette without becoming visually heavy.

Roof Movement

The stair roof study and dome references reinforce the project's interest in continuous spans, controlled curves, and elegant overhead form.

Parametric Influence

The studies point toward a hospitality language shaped by repetition, light filtering, and structural rhythm rather than applied ornament.

Showcase

The hotel reads as a quiet resort landscape with a luminous urban presence.

The renderings move from large site perspectives to closer facade and lighting views so the project is understood both as a destination plan and as a guest-facing architectural experience.

Drawings

Technical sheets organize the hotel through circulation, rooms, and section depth.

The drawing set reads from plan to elevation and section, clarifying the guest sequence, room organization, landscape edges, and the layered envelope strategies that support the project's hospitality character.
